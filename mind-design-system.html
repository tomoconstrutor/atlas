Layout Rules

Stack one word 5× at line-height: 0.88 — lines compress intentionally

Four corner anchors frame the composition — labels mirror left/right

Flat type is inert — never animated, never hovered

06 Motion

Rotate linear · 4s

Motion Principles

Blobs move · type stays

The flat typographic stack is the anchor — immovable. Blobs breathe, float, and react. Motion reinforces the material hierarchy.

Gravity · Physics

Entrances use overshoot easing — spring physics. Blobs feel weighted, not mechanical. Cast shadows shift slightly during float loops.

Duration Scale

Hover: 200–300ms. Idle float: 4–5s. Page entrance: 600–900ms with staggered delays. Nothing snaps; nothing lingers.

Parallax Layer

On scroll, blobs move at 0.7× the scroll rate vs. flat type — subtle depth without full 3D perspective transforms.
